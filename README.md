# Nexo

**Nexo — conectando quem precisa a quem pode fazer.**

Nexo is a marketplace for mobility, deliveries, cargo transport, towing and future service categories.

## Business model
- Provider subscription: **R$99.90/month**
- **0% commission** on completed rides or services
- Provider keeps **100% of the service value**

## Initial passenger pricing
- Car: **R$2.00/km**
- Motorcycle: **R$1.50/km**

## Initial payment methods
- Physical cash
- Pix
- Credit/debit card using the provider's own card machine

## Core product
- Passenger/customer profiles
- Driver/provider profiles
- Photos, videos and captions
- Bilateral reputation
- Identity and vehicle verification
- Passenger transport
- Deliveries
- Cargo transport with negotiation
- Towing
- Provider subscriptions
