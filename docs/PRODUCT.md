# Nexo — Product Principles

## Promise
**Nexo — conectando quem precisa a quem pode fazer.**

## Positioning
Nexo is not intended to be merely another ride-hailing app. It is a digital work platform for independent providers and a trusted marketplace for customers.

## Provider value
- R$99.90/month
- 0% commission
- 100% of service value remains with provider
- professional profile
- reputation
- customer relationships
- multiple service categories

## Customer value
- transparent passenger pricing
- provider identity/vehicle information
- reputation
- choice where supported
- recurring providers
- multiple service categories

## Core loop
Need → Match → Trust → Contract → Pay → Rate → Repeat

## Product guardrails
Do not add features merely because competitors have them. A feature should improve connection, trust, execution, retention or marketplace liquidity.

## Growth hypothesis
The strongest initial wedge is a concentrated local marketplace with enough provider supply and customer demand to make the subscription visibly valuable.

## Key business risk
The primary risk is marketplace liquidity. A provider will not retain a monthly subscription without sufficient demand. Product and launch strategy must therefore prioritize real customer demand, not only provider acquisition.
